# Data coverage and source register

Recorded dataset-wide review date: **2026-10-25** (مهر ۱۴۰۵). This is inherited metadata, not a claim that every individual route or value was independently re-verified on that date.

See [Migration research and data-update policy](migration-research-methodology.md) for the source hierarchy, reviewed global data portals, field-level evidence standard, and versioned/offline update safeguards. The source library is curated and non-exhaustive; global statistics are kept separate from legal eligibility rules.

### Added destination source notes (checked 2026-11-01)

- **Ireland:** Critical Skills and General Employment permit criteria and the salary-threshold roadmap are published by DETE; student and STEP information is published by Immigration Service Delivery. The 2026 general pay figures are route/category-specific, not universal eligibility tests. Sources: [Critical Skills](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/permit-types/critical-skills-employment-permit/), [General Employment](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/permit-types/general-employment-permit/), [2026 salary roadmap](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/news-and-events/department-news/2025/december/20251202.html), [STEP](https://www.irishimmigration.ie/coming-to-work-in-ireland/what-are-my-options-for-working-in-ireland/coming-to-work-for-more-than-90-days/start-up-entrepreneur-programme-step/).
- **Portugal:** The national visa portal lists work, highly qualified activity, remote work, business and study routes. Remote-work evidence requires average monthly income over the previous three months at four times the minimum guaranteed monthly remuneration; the official 2026 subsistence page lists a EUR 920 minimum wage. The former job-seeker route stopped accepting applications on 23 October 2025; the replacement skilled job-seeker route was not yet open pending regulation, so it is not presented as available in the app. Sources: [visa types](https://vistos.mne.gov.pt/en/national-visas/general-information/type-of-visa), [residency documents](https://vistos.mne.gov.pt/en/national-visas/necessary-documentation/residency), [means of subsistence](https://vistos.mne.gov.pt/en/national-visas/necessary-documentation/means-of-subsistence), [official notice on the job-seeker change](https://viena.embaixadaportugal.mne.gov.pt/en/the-embassy/news/change-in-the-regime-regarding-work-seeking-visa-applications).
- **Finland:** Migri's 2026 guidance gives EUR 3,937/month for specialists, a general EUR 1,600/month floor for employed persons (with collective-agreement pay potentially higher), and EUR 800/month for the principal student applicant, excluding tuition and dependant requirements. Sources: [specialist permit](https://migri.fi/en/specialist), [income requirements](https://migri.fi/en/working-in-finland/income-requirement), [student funds](https://migri.fi/en/income-requirement-for-students), [startup permit](https://migri.fi/en/start-up-entrepreneur).
- **Belgium:** A single permit for non-EEA workers is employer-led and routed through the competent region; EU Blue Card salary thresholds and self-employed permissions also require regional checks. Sources: [Working in Belgium](https://www.workinginbelgium.be/en/home.html), [Belgian EU Blue Card](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/eu-immigration-portal/eu-blue-card/eu-blue-card-belgium_en), [self-employed route](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/eu-immigration-portal/self-employed-worker-belgium_en), [Immigration Office](https://dofi.ibz.be/en), [study](https://dofi.ibz.be/en/themes/third-country-nationals/study).

The four added country records were checked on the date above. This does **not** change the dataset-wide review date or claim that all pre-existing countries and routes were re-verified. App scores for the new routes are comparative triage only; the official source controls eligibility, fees and processing times.


### China, Qatar, Japan, South Korea, Malaysia and Singapore (checked 2026-11-01)

- **China:** Official NIA guidance distinguishes work, study and residence procedures; the Chinese Embassy's visa-category page identifies Z (work), X1 (long-term study) and R (high-level/needed talent). Q1 family reunion applies only to eligible relatives and is not a general family visa. The NIA guidance page is older, so current local documents and processes must be confirmed before filing. Sources: [NIA entry/residence guidance](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147423/n147478/n147715/c158215/content.html), [visa category definitions](https://no.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/lsfw_0/qzyw/202309/t20230905_11138640.htm).
- **Qatar:** Hukoomi describes employer-arranged work/residence and general visa pathways. The current Invest Qatar/Jusour Residency Program page gives the entrepreneur incubator-endorsement and bank-statement conditions, plus a separate executive stream limited to current QID holders with employer nomination, five years of senior management experience, and title-specific salary floors (QAR 50,000 or QAR 80,000). App scoring does not verify sponsor eligibility, QID, endorsement or every title-specific condition. Sources: [work/residence permits](https://portal.www.gov.qa/wps/wcm/connect/hukoomi+web+content/hukoomi/topics/visas+and+official+documents/residenceandworkpermits), [visa guidance](https://portal.www.gov.qa/wps/wcm/connect/hukoomi+web+content/hukoomi/topics/visas+and+official+documents/visas), [Residency Program](https://www.invest.qa/en/residency-program).
- **Japan:** ISA publishes the HSP framework, Engineer/Specialist status, SSW conditions (designated sectors, skills/language tests and category-specific limits), and student status. The official HSP threshold is 70 points; Visayar's comparative score is explicitly not that calculation. Sources: [HSP](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/status/designatedactivities02_00004.html?lang=en), [Engineer/Specialist](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/status/gijinkoku.html), [Specified Skilled Worker](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/content/930004452.pdf), [Student](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/status/student.html?lang=en).
- **South Korea:** The Ministry of Justice Visa Navigator found for D-2, D-10, E-7 and D-8 is dated 2022. It is used only to identify route categories; the pack does not encode fresh salary/funds thresholds from that guide. Applicants must confirm current eligibility and the relevant consular/immigration notice. Source: [Visa Navigator (English)](https://www.immigration.go.kr/bbs/immigration_eng/230/454085/download.do).
- **Malaysia:** The Employment Pass source announces a revised expatriate salary policy effective 1 June 2026; the app avoids guessing a universal salary floor because policy category and contract details matter. DE Rantau FAQ v10 lists annual income thresholds of USD 24,000 for tech talent and USD 60,000 for non-tech talent. MM2H is multi-tiered; no single financial threshold is generalized across tiers. Sources: [revised Employment Pass salary policy](https://esd.imi.gov.my/portal/pdf/Revised_Expatriate_Salary_Policy.pdf), [DE Rantau FAQ v10](https://www.mdec.my/api/media/file/DE-Rantau_Pass_FAQ_v10.pdf), [MM2H](https://www.imi.gov.my/index.php/en/main-services/malaysia-my-second-home-mmh2-en/).
- **Singapore:** MOM's current 2026 eligibility pages list EP base qualifying salary from SGD 5,600/month (SGD 6,200 in financial services) and S Pass from SGD 3,300/month (SGD 3,800 in financial services), both rising with age; future increases effective 2027 are noted by MOM. COMPASS, employer quota/levy and exemptions still apply. EntrePass has alternative founder criteria, not a universal startup-capital threshold; ICA requires admission to an eligible full-time course for Student's Pass. Sources: [EP](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/eligibility), [S Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/eligibility), [EntrePass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/entrepass/eligibility), [ONE Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/eligibility), [Student's Pass](https://www.ica.gov.sg/reside/STP/apply).

The six new country records carry their own `official.lastVerified` date, **2026-11-01**. The dataset-wide review date remains **2026-10-25**: it is not advanced by this scoped expansion. New-country scores are comparative triage only. Official gates are modeled only where the current source and available profile fields support them; some key conditions (such as Qatar QID, incubator endorsement, Singapore COMPASS, and Japanese language/occupation tests) remain manual checks and are called out in route notes.

## 4. Occupations

`shared/dataset.json → occupations` holds 50 curated occupations with Canadian
**NOC 2021** code, **TEER** level, Australian **ANZSCO** code, bilingual titles and
a demand list per destination. Codes are indicative; the app shows them as
identifiers so an applicant can confirm against the official NOC/ANZSCO editions
before filing.

## 5. Monthly human review and release controls

The repository opens a monthly GitHub issue containing every country and route
source (`.github/workflows/monthly-data-review.yml`). This is a reminder and
checklist, not an automated source monitor: migration rules are never scraped,
changed, or published without a human reviewer. Dataset-wide `meta.lastVerified`
and each country's `official.lastVerified` are review metadata; **they do not prove
that each individual fee, criterion, or route was independently checked on that
date**. Some country records inherit the dataset-wide date, while separately
reviewed country packs may carry their own date. Reviewers must link the exact
official page/notice and record its publication or effective date in the review
PR. Where a source is ambiguous or unavailable, leave the current value
unchanged and document the uncertainty.

For a completed review:

1. Check all 25 country portals and all 100 route source links, including fees,
   eligibility, points/thresholds, occupation lists, language rules, quotas,
   funds and effective dates. Re-check estimates and their `verified` markers.
2. Change only values confirmed to have changed. Add the official source, notice
   date, effective date and reviewer evidence to the PR; do not infer or guess.
3. Update `LAST_VERIFIED` in `tools/dataset_lib.py` only after the scheduled
   full scope has actually been reviewed. The builder propagates it as the
   dataset-wide date and default country date; separately reviewed country packs
   may set an explicit date, which must only be advanced after their own review.
4. Run `python3 tools/build_dataset.py`, `node tools/sync_mobile_data.js`,
   `python3 tools/check_dataset_freshness.py`, `python3 tools/make_fixtures.py`,
   `node tools/parity_check.js`, `node tools/check_i18n.js`, and the backend and
   mobile test suites. Obtain human approval before merge.
5. Increment `meta.version` for each published dataset content change; keep
   `schemaVersion` for client-contract changes. The API computes a content ETag
   from the complete dataset; mobile clients revalidate on launch and cache the
   latest valid dataset for offline use. A new APK is required once to install
   this sync capability; later reviewed datasets can be delivered by the API
   without an app reinstall.

Dataset metadata advertises a 30-day review interval and becomes stale after
45 days. The app shows the recorded review date dynamically in Persian or
English and displays a monthly-review reminder when due, followed by a stronger
stale warning. The Android release workflow blocks a release when stale; the
freshness checker never changes the recorded date or data itself. The monthly
issue is the routine to complete before the 45-day release gate.
